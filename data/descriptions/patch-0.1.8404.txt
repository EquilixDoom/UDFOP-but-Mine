Boons ||| Boons is the fourth Climates & Calories setting, between Off and Casual, and the default. It takes nothing away: keeping the character fed, watered and rested earns bonuses instead. Well fed gives +5 Strength and +5 Endurance, Quenched +5 Agility and +5 Speed, Well rested +5 Intelligence and +5 Willpower (sleeping in a bed or by a fire banks up to 12 hours of rest that is spent while awake), and Thriving, all three at once, +5 Luck, while Hearth gives stamina back when standing by a lit fire. Boons show as green tiles on the status strip and the Status page lists the banked rest as its own line.
Campfire ||| The Campfire is a camping item with eight nights of fuel. Placing it is free and sleeping at one uses one night, and when it burns down it goes cold and stays cold until relit with fuel or picked up. Looking at a fire or tent offers Rest here, Cook food, Relight and Pick up. It costs 40 gold, half that online, and General Stores and Pawn Shops never sell out of them. One left in a dungeon comes out with the player, and campfires and tents on a boat's deck stay on the boat as she sails. Resting at the player's own burnt-out Campfire relights it if it has fuel, resting at a brazier or fire bowl needs the player within the fire's reach (from further away they are told to move closer), a cold camp no longer offers Cook, and a campfire on a ship's deck no longer counts as a fire while the ship is out of sight.
Climates & Calories ||| Climates and Calories is a survival mod with Off, Boons, Casual and Hard settings, Boons being the default. Hunger and thirst have staged need tiles (such as 2/3 for Hungry), rations in the pack are eaten automatically when Hungry, and a tavern drink quenches thirst fully. Inns and taverns refill waterskins at the price of the cheapest soft drink and each region serves its own menu. Indoors the temperature is never worse than the street, in hot weather clothes count for half their warmth, and coming out of Scorching or Freezing tells the player. Corpse food works online. Felt temperature follows the original mod more closely: Resist Fire no longer makes a blizzard colder nor Resist Frost the desert hotter, swimming and rain and snow soak at the mod's rates (a cloak keeps snow off and a hood rain), robes and cloaks keep the sun off armour, underground the dungeon's air rules clothes, armour and food, and the open sea is cold in winter. Offline a long rest or fast-travel journey feels each hour as it passes, and in Hard a long journey can no longer collapse the player over and over or kill them on landing, collapsing counts as an hour's sleep on the ground, and saving mid-journey is refused. Leaving prison the player starts fed, watered and rested, a cured vampire is not starving, a werewolf in beast form neither hungers nor thirsts, and food that spoils shows its spoiled name. New characters start with the mod's Formal cloak and Shoes, both well worn, tavern menus follow its regional menus, prices and holiday free drinks, a meal ordered when nearly full is served whole, a soft drink gives +5 stamina and an alcoholic drink restores stamina and magicka, and online passing out drunk wakes the player at the next dawn.
Climbing ||| Climbing lets the player pull up onto ledges, wall tops, parapets and roofs by pressing Jump at them, and hang from edges. Climbing skill sets reach, speed, grip time and how long a fall can still be caught, with every point past 100 making the player faster up to about 1.4 times at 200. From the ground a shoulder-height ledge is reachable at skill 0 and one above the head at 100, a full pack lowers reach, and a roof can be climbed from its eave up to 50 degrees. With Roleplay and Realism on, the player cannot climb while holding a weapon, and online others see the player on the wall and hear them climb. Climbing is not possible while fast travelling on the Overworld, a journey cannot be started while hanging on a wall, and climbing works as before at walking pace. In first person (classic view) the hands stay on the wall when climbing or hanging, sliding across with the stone as the view turns and going down the wall when looking up, and the two fists grip about shoulder width apart. When looking away from the wall to find the next jump, the arm on the side looked reaches out and stays fixed to the screen while its hand lets go, and the other hand keeps its hold.
Crime ||| A crime is now charged once. Running from one group of guards and meeting another no longer charges it twice, and a crime committed while fleeing the watch can no longer reach the court uncharged and raise the player's standing with its sentence. Pleading guilty no longer loops: the plea waits until the Crime tutorial page on top of it has been read, so each plea is answered once and the fine is taken and the sentence halved only once.
Duels ||| Duels are the player-versus-player foundation where fights are judged by the server against actual weapons and gear, using a separate duel vitality bar that leaves real health untouched, showing both bars from the start and recording wins and losses from server results. Melee and ranged attacks in duels, sieges and the Wrothgarian Mountains share the same damage ceiling as spells, at most 180 damage in any 5 seconds counting blows and casts together. Boons and other buffs count properly in duels, sieges and the Royal Tourney.
Front Door ||| There is one front door under both skins, and you can now decorate the ground right outside your own front door. On a handheld held sideways, such as the ASUS ROG Xbox Ally X, the logo no longer fills the screen and the menu keeps its two columns, the sections down the side and the open page at full height, and a narrowed desktop browser window gets the same layout.
Horse ||| Horses never die, can be fed to grow the player's bond, must be kept fed, and the player can own more than one. A horse walks by default and holding Run (Shift unless rebound) gallops it, as on foot. The gallop is as fast as before and spends the horse's breath, and a winded or starving horse walks even with Run held until it can gallop again. Holding Run gallops it anywhere, in town or out, as long as it has the breath, online too, though riding into someone at a gallop tramples them, which is a crime and brings the guards. A walking horse has a slower clop, a slower head nod in first person and a trot on the 3D horse, and the saddle panel says Walking. With Horse care off in Settings > Features, or on the classic interface, horses ride as they always did. A horse with a new coat met on the road no longer freezes the game.
Language ||| Settings > Interface > Language offers 13 languages besides English: Bulgarian, Czech, Danish, French, German, Indonesian, Italian, Korean, Norwegian, Portuguese (Brazil), Romanian, Russian and Spanish. The choice applies at once and is remembered, and if the browser reads one of them first the game offers it once in that language. The translations cover the game's own menus and screens (the front door and Settings, the pause window, the death screens, account and online messages, the Professions and Stores pages and every crafting station, the Features screen and every mod's settings) and are machine translated, which the game says. The classic windows draw any script, including Cyrillic, Greek, Vietnamese, Chinese, Japanese and Korean, and the About pane credits each translation.
Morrowind Body ||| The Morrowind body features climbing poses (hanging, shimmying, wall climbing, leaps, pull-overs, vault tucks), gripping animations, first-person hand reach, crowd optimization, port-made steel plate armor sets, synchronized attack speeds, spell-casting animations, and T-pose fixes. Placed home/yard decoration NPCs stand in Morrowind bodies, you can ride in third person, look down over the 3D horse's neck in first person, and 3D horses match sprite coats with unique flank markings. With the Morrowind model in first person the arms grip the wall while climbing even with the weapon sheathed.
Morrowind Models ||| Morrowind models bring the Morrowind rig into the game with range-based asset loading, parity audits, faster loads, physical dropped items with custom pictures/models, ground rarity outlines/lights, physics collisions, smooth shading across all body/armour/weapon parts, and fixed third-person horse riding view. Morrowind models (the player, NPCs, enemies and horses) no longer turn invisible until restart. If one body gets a bad position online, only that body stays at its last good position instead of every body drawn after it vanishing.
Resting ||| Online, the player rests at a lit fire, a bed (room, house, ship's cabin or dungeon bed on their floor) or a guild hall that allows it. They hold still six seconds, the rest breaks when an enemy comes, they are hurt or the fire goes out, and when the bar fills they sleep a whole night, eight hours on their own clock, in one step, healing health, stamina and magicka as it fills. A night comes once every ten minutes of play, resting sooner being a short rest that heals and passes no time, and the enhanced HUD's status bar shows a Rested tile counting minutes to the next night. A night at a fire, tent or bed heals fully while a guild hall floor is rough, monsters can still find the player at night, and the loiter option is gone online since the sky runs on real time. Resting ages spells, diseases, hunger and skill checks as an hour of rest should. Resting while poisoned or diseased warns the player first. Online, the tile counting down until the player can sleep through a night again is called "Next night", not "Rested".
Temperature Display ||| A temperature plate at the right end of the compass (Enhanced HUD) shows the felt temperature, its word and a scale from deadly cold to scorching, staying on screen at every HUD scale and on phones, movable and hideable on its own and still shown when the compass is hidden; "Temperature on the HUD" in the Climates & Calories settings turns it off. The Enhanced inventory shows the felt temperature beside the armour rating and an item's card says what wearing or removing it would do, the Stats side lists everything that makes up the temperature and the Status page shows the number. Red means the setting played on charges for it and amber means it is felt but costs nothing, and each level is also said in words.
Tents ||| Pitching a tent is free, and a night slept at the player's own tent uses one of its uses. Sleeping in the tent keeps the rain and snow off, and armour no longer rusts while resting or under a roof.
Translation Packs ||| Under the Language row, Translation pack installs a Daggerfall Unity translation (a folder or its .zip) from the player's own files, uploading nothing. A pack translates Daggerfall's own text too, dialogue, quests, books and the names of places, enemies, items and spells, and works for a language not yet in the list; the French pack's grammar works.
Water Surface ||| The enhanced water surface covers oceans, rivers and ponds, with a basin, swell, foam and puddles that draw and swim. It is darker and less see-through, the shore is the art's own outline exactly and the water is the art's own, with shallows at 0.80 opacity and ?water=mask for debugging. At night water takes a quarter of a light diffusely and shows each light as a highlight, and it was audited. From high ground the far sea no longer fades into a pale band of fog ending in a hard square edge, but runs on through the haze to the horizon in one piece, with the same colour, light and sun glints as the water near the player and the sky reflected as the near water does, at no extra cost. Inland water banks (ponds, moats, rivers, streams and town canals) no longer show flat blue blotches on the grass, the shore's painted water sitting under the riverbed with the strip past the edge reading as dark wet mud, and a player on a bank can no longer see under the surface because the water's edge meets the ground.
Weapon Widget ||| Weapon Widget is a mod, version 1.6, ported one to one. Its idle bob, widget texture door and draw seam were fixed, its defaults and preset reset repaired, and Inertia now sways the sprites when the player looks around at the intended strength. Its bob and sway no longer push the Morrowind hands off the stone while they climb.
