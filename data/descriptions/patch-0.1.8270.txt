Horse ||| Horses never die, can be fed to grow the player's bond, must be kept fed, and the player can own more than one. A horse walks by default and holding Run (Shift unless rebound) gallops it, as on foot. The gallop is as fast as before and spends the horse's breath, and a winded or starving horse walks even with Run held until it can gallop again. In towns the horse walks because Roleplay & Realism does not allow galloping there, unless the Galloping In Towns option under Enhanced Riding is on. A walking horse has a slower clop, a slower head nod in first person and a trot on the 3D horse, and the saddle panel says Walking. With Horse care off in Settings > Features, or on the classic interface, horses ride as they always did. A horse with a new coat met on the road no longer freezes the game.
