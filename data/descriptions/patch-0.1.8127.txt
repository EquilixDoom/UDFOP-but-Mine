Argonian ||| Argonian is a rendering topic[cite: 1]. Its recorded changes fix an Argonian shown on a human Morrowind body, and now khajiit and Argonians go barefoot and bareheaded, as Morrowind dresses them[cite: 1].
Character Creation ||| Character creation is the screen where a new character is made[cite: 1]. The creator tells the player what they rolled, a phone version exists, and a character made online no longer gets a skill check the moment it is created[cite: 1]. The Project Legacy question (Enduring, Bloodline, No lineage) and the leveling question are Enhanced Plus popups in which the option under the pointer is the one highlighted and clicking it selects it[cite: 1]. Additionally, heads and hairs are now paired freely, from every pairing of the heads and hairs your Morrowind data offers a race at character creation, whereas there were previously ten[cite: 1].
Character Sheet ||| The character sheet page has one door and includes a setting under Settings → Interface → Character sheet for Every faction's standing (off by default) to list reputation with every guild, temple, knightly order, kingdom, witches' coven, vampire clan and Daedric Prince[cite: 1]. The Standing page now lists your reputation with every guild, temple, knightly order, kingdom, coven, vampire clan and Daedric Prince by default, not only the ones you belong to[cite: 1].
DFO Grimoire ||| DFO Grimoire is a fourth look under Main menu > Overhauls > UI, dressing everything Enhanced Plus does in GrimoireUI's parchment, wood, red-and-gold presses, glass vials and lettering[cite: 1]. The vitals are three vials, upright in the corner in Daggerfall's order or lying in a row under the hotbar, and the card offers Pages, Backdrop, Vitals, and Spellbook choices[cite: 1]. The front door stands on a detailed desk, rarity/set/sigil names are inked deeper, parchment text is readable, stray ruled lines are removed, health bars scale with resolution, and settings open as scrolls[cite: 1]. New features include a Text font setting under Overhauls > UI with five choices (Game, Sans, Monospace, Dyslexic, My font)[cite: 1], distinct channel colours with colour squares and toggle options[cite: 1], player name colours[cite: 1], language channels in 15 languages with `/lang` and `/lc` commands[cite: 1], gold-highlighted `@mentions` with chimes[cite: 1], unread count seals on Grimoire tabs[cite: 1], and various UI/inventory/scry fixes[cite: 1].
Desktop App ||| The desktop app is the downloadable version of the game, with saves as real files, built on Electron 42[cite: 1]. The Windows installer and the Linux AppImage update themselves, the portable exe and the Mac tell the player when a new version is out with a Download button, the app has its own icon, and it finds and installs updates again[cite: 1]. It is not yet code-signed[cite: 1]. Updating no longer leaves the game missing or unpacks 18,000 files every launch[cite: 1]. Downloads are named DFO-Online-... from the rename on, and an installed copy finds its update as usual[cite: 1]. On a Mac the new version installs as DFO Online beside the old Daggerfall Online app (to be dragged to the Bin once open), and on Windows pinned taskbar or Start menu entries may need repinning under the new name[cite: 1].
Developers ||| RookieG became a developer in 0.1.6491[cite: 1], and the chapters from "The Chapters" update are now open to everyone[cite: 1].
Elites ||| Elites are tougher special foes[cite: 1]. In the open world 5% of foes stand as elites, with no limit of one at a time, after a brief spell at 2% that was undone[cite: 1]. They appear only from foe level 3 and never as city watch or allies, ordinary dungeons hold one 20% of the time, and Elite Dungeons keep their 3 to 4[cite: 1]. Elites are named everywhere (on hover, death notices, corpses)[cite: 1], appear offline while Loot rarity is on, and level-1 or level-2 characters meet none[cite: 1]. Additionally, the Greater Giant in the open zone drops its proper elite-table loot again (magic/common pieces, a chance at rare/legendary, and gold by level)[cite: 1].
Enhanced Plus ||| Enhanced Plus had a pre-merge audit in 0.1.4290[cite: 1]. It allows you to check your horse's status (breath, hunger with hours left, bond, gait, speed) and perform panel actions (feed oats, give apple, use repair kit, open wagon cargo, open stable page, switch view, dismount) without menus or keys[cite: 1]. Team hunger tracks the hungriest horse, unavailable buttons are greyed out with hovers explaining why, and you can free the mouse (Y) or tap on a phone[cite: 1].
Etheric Sea ||| Sailing west of the Bay's mouth on the Abecean asks whether to sail into the open sea, leading to Veilhaven three days later, while sailing back asks the way home with warnings if enemies/players are close[cite: 1]. Past the Veil lies an endless sea of isles (port isles with towns/quays, ruins with dungeons, wild isles with goods, and bare rocks)[cite: 1], growing deadlier and richer further west through Shallows, Reaches, Deep Aether, and Abyss[cite: 1]. No crown's law reaches past the Veil (no navy, crimes aren't counted, corsairs sail under sea names)[cite: 1], ether-amber, starglass, and voidsilk are traded, wild isles float casks and refill in seven days[cite: 1], six legendary ships haunt the depths with purses[cite: 1], and the travel map key opens a chart of sighted isles[cite: 1]. The Veil blocks magic (Recall, teleports, portal stones, party travel, Fleet ships) and falling wakes players at the nearest port isle[cite: 1]. The light is cold and violet, the sea west of the Bay is water again[cite: 1], and while classic keeps Daggerfall's sea, enhanced/online play features this crossing[cite: 1].
Greater Giant ||| The Greater Giant now hits every player at four times damage[cite: 1].
Guild Hall ||| A guild hall is a guild-owned house bought by the guildmaster with treasury funds, featuring member chests, crafting, resting, customizable painting and yard furnishings, and a hall's roll as the first book on its shelf detailing its chapter's standing, Master, and officers[cite: 1]. Windows inside buildings are now see-through[cite: 1], town boards fill in once three players have visited guild halls[cite: 1], and in rooms with many lamps, stationary people cast shadows from every lamp rather than just the nearest twelve[cite: 1].
Guilds ||| Guilds are player organisations founded on Renown 10 with restrictions on disbanding and standing reviews, functioning properly across regions, and online you can set entry permissions like an online home (Only me, My party, My guild, or Anyone)[cite: 1]. Townsfolk, shopkeepers, guild members, nobles and priests are dressed from a wardrobe of their own in Morrowind garments and colours, while a fighters' guild's people wear whatever armour came to hand[cite: 1].
Harbours ||| Port towns feature moored ships with stowed sails that arrive and depart, merchantmen, patrolling navy ships, and offshore pirates[cite: 1]. Coastal seats can raise a Harbour[cite: 1], berths and quays lie further from shore where the sea floor shelves gently[cite: 1], harbours far from streets don't constantly search for docks[cite: 1], lake confusion is fixed[cite: 1], ports/lighthouses/quays run lighter[cite: 1], and docks no longer cross each other[cite: 1].
Homes ||| Homes have a Who may enter setting, including My guild[cite: 1]. Room furniture can be taken out, knightly order free houses are never owned by other players, graveyard crypts are not sold as homes online, and online homes/guild halls on the arena's block move to new houses on login without emptying storage[cite: 1]. A phantom room tab in two-storey homes (often "Room 3") has been fixed[cite: 1].
Horse ||| The weapon over the horse was fixed[cite: 1]. Horses never die, you can gallop on their breath, feed them to grow your bond, must keep them fed, and can own more than one horse[cite: 1].
Horse Cart and Cargo ||| Horse Cart and Cargo is a mod ported off its IL, with parked teams appearing online and on the ground, and other riders visible[cite: 1]. Other riders on Morrowind bodies, mod horses, and road wagon teams are all 3D horses too[cite: 1].
Lighthouses ||| Port town lighthouses feature whitewashed stone towers with red bands, glass lantern rooms, copper domes, and keeper's cottages[cite: 1]. Beacons are lit dusk to dawn, two beams sweep the sea/town, and doors/keepers allow climbing to galleries on foot without falling hazards[cite: 1]. Lighthouse and shipyard office windows have small leaded panes[cite: 1], and a tutorial page exists[cite: 1].
Lighting ||| Lighting covers the world's light model with moonlight/night directional terms, clustered lights, horizon-based occlusion, glow, distant fog, audits, limitations, and overhead lamp shadows[cite: 1]. A torch, lantern or Candle spell underwater no longer whites out the screen, and lights in heavy fog and sandstorms glow slightly less[cite: 1].

Player actions (kindness, help, theft, crime) affect social standing and permanent household hostility[cite: 1].
