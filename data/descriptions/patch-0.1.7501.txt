A Living Hall ||| A thriving chapter's hall is busier, with its members gathering there more evenings each week, while a failing hall stands half empty.
Cloaks ||| Casual and Formal Cloaks are now real cloaks worn over your armour instead of a full robe that hid it, hanging from the shoulders and swinging with your legs as you walk with smooth shoulders and individual colours for each dye.
Ebony Armour ||| Mithril and Adamantium still wear the Morrowind ebony look.
Maps ||| New setting: Settings → Interface → Maps → Map text and icons - Auto, or 0.75× to 2×.
Orrery of Endings ||| The six stones are rebuilt as carved shafts with their sign, a dial in a bezel, and a gear on top that turns with the hand, while six rings turn slowly overhead, shivering as the Hour frays.
Receipts ||| A chapter's receipt writ gives +2 more for your first such deed of the day in that region.
Spellcasting ||| The casting hands are quick again so a spell leaves your hands 0.2 seconds after you cast it as before the casting speed change, spells still can't be spammed, and the thin dotted line that showed up beside the casting hands on some graphics cards is gone.
Stepping Through ||| Entering the Hour has its own transition where twelve brass blades close over the Rift wherever it is on your screen and lock into a clock face (holding still and fading in and out if set to reduce motion), and when you arrive the way back breathes out a spray of brass sparks and its ring turns once.
The Caravan ||| Craft at stations, rest, and a save or Recall made inside a caravan that can't be re-entered now puts you outside its rear door.
The Rift ||| Its iris shows its state (opening wide while the Hour can be entered, narrowing to a pinhole when it can't, and shutting when the Hour closes), and through the iris you see the Hour's own sky with real depth as you move.
The Shattered Hour ||| The islands hang in the void on jagged rock spires with half-buried gears and chains swaying on the clock's tick, the way back looks into the hall you came from in dark coursed stone lit gold from the front, the arena tells you the fight through its floor, the Stomp's ring rises as a low wall of brass light you can see to jump, the Remnant shows what it's about to do, and when it falls its heart tears out and rises before going dark while the dial breaks free and rolls away.
The Unmoored Steps ||| Crumble stones crack in stages under your feet, fall in pieces, and fly back together when they return, a vane on the last island swings toward the next gust a second before it hits, and waystones mark where the void will send you back, playing a golden burst in reverse when it does.
Battle ||| Battles at a seat can be left with Leave, Close, /leave, walking out or dying, each battle is announced in red at the Turning a day, an hour and five minutes before, and fights in the zone are judged by the server the same way duels are.
Beautiful Villages ||| Beautiful Villages fixes floating scenery so trees stand on the ground drawn beneath them, houses and the stone gazebo no longer float as hills are drawn at their real size and shape, and a house in Akatosh's temple villages buried inside a hill now stands on open ground nearby where quests lead to it.
Burning Court ||| The Burning Court is a world feature where the bridge portal is replaced by a fire gate that opens and closes for players, the way home opens upon the Warden's fall and closes as the court comes apart, the only ways out until he falls are death and the Wrath with /unstuck disabled, and stalled rooms return players to Tamriel after 20 seconds.
Character Sheet ||| The character sheet page has one door and includes a setting under Settings → Interface → Character sheet for Every faction's standing (off by default) to list reputation with every guild, temple, knightly order, kingdom, witches' coven, vampire clan and Daedric Prince.
Climb ||| Climb received a fix for the climb over the lip, and every step now shows what it will do next with beat plates as dissolving clock faces, drift steps hanging from pendulums, and risers climbing on gear racks.
Compass ||| The compass marks things around the player including nearby ships, gathering spots within 150 m in profession colours, and placed boats as teal markers, working on Classic and Enhanced compasses and the Overworld view, with redone lighting where floors read as stone and brass instead of amber.
Crafting Stations ||| Crafting stations use the Enhanced Plus look with diamond strikes for mini-games like the anvil's heat, loom's beat, and workbench's plane, and every station now uses materials in your Materials Bag and pack as well as your Stores.
Death Screen ||| The death screen was fixed and audited, including resolving the online screen's rise and ensuring quick-loading (F11) after dying in town no longer leaves the death screen up.
Decor ||| Decor items like candles and pieces on a dresser can be clicked past the mirror, and you can place decor.
Duels ||| Duels are the player-versus-player foundation where fights are judged by the server against actual weapons and gear, using a separate duel vitality bar that leaves real health untouched, showing both bars from the start and recording wins and losses from server results.
Guild Hall ||| A guild hall is a guild-owned house bought by the guildmaster with treasury funds, featuring member chests, crafting, resting, customizable painting and yard furnishings, and a hall's roll as the first book on its shelf detailing its chapter's standing, Master, and officers.
Guilds ||| Guilds are player organisations founded on Renown 10 with restrictions on disbanding and standing reviews, functioning properly across regions, and online you can set entry permissions like an online home (Only me, My party, My guild, or Anyone).
Hall of Records ||| Every seat's palace and boards keep its whole Chronicle as a book readable each Season, recording every seat that changed hands, Season ending, and patron.
Hanging ||| Hanging allows holding ledges, climbing, shimmying around corners and towers, and climbing down or dropping off, complemented in the Shattered Hour by a hand-painted sky featuring void bands, stars, aurorae, a star-drawn clock face with Endings constellations, and hanging Bay cities.
Helm ||| The helm's black box was fixed, the Brass Remnant was rebuilt with riveted brass plate, and the steel helm (closed and open) now sits properly on your head.
Influence ||| Influence is how guilds compete for regional seats through weekly pledges, the Watch, gate closures, Renown XP, member homes, and silver tribute, though patronage buys no influence over seats.
Logging ||| Logging is a gathering profession where wilderness trees are felled using a Wood-Axe and clean cuts with timber going to Stores, supporting all forest types and online visibility beyond the Bay.
Mining ||| Mining is a gathering profession utilizing wilderness ore veins, dungeon walls, and boulder quarrying with Pick-Axes and glint strikes, featuring region-specific ores and progression tied to confirmed land and player gathering activity.
Mods ||| The Mods pane was replaced by a unified system with every mod enabled, creator titles, one search across every tab, and a Mods & files section listing every mod with direct access to options.
Morrowind Asset Pack ||| The Morrowind asset pack features an On/Off row, removal door, asset card, and an updated steel armour model with wider breastplate shoulders, larger pauldrons, and an independent waist band piece.
Motherlodes ||| Motherlodes are daily rich ore heaps announced in chat and marked on the compass, requiring Mining 25 and Pick-Axe strikes for ore, XP, and silver without counting against harvest limits, and neither motherlodes nor veins, boulders, herbs, and trees appear inside rocks.
Oblivion Gates ||| Oblivion Gates are nightly raid events featuring a handcrafted model of cracked basalt and fire with multi-court Warden boss fights, damage charts, and rewards, alongside tavern card games like Iliac Hand played against regulars or online players for stakes and collection cards.
Online Characters ||| Creating an online character starts the game directly, and server-side save auditing checks items, attributes, and wealth growth against playtime while safeguarding player progress.
Online Foes ||| Online enemies receive damage properly and feature improved movement synchronization so players are less likely to swing at empty air, alongside enhanced rendering for summoned and called monsters.

Realm ||| The realm system provides multiplayer features and table interactions where the UI clearly explains buy-in stake restrictions at tables.

Royal Tourney ||| Royal Tourneys are crown-proclaimed weekly ladder events held in castle duel rings with server-refereed physical blows, prize pools, and server-capped spell restrictions.

Seats ||| Seat towns feature palace charters, map markers, and kingdom banners, with chapters featuring officer and Master ranks determined by accumulated merit, reputation, and tenure to provide unique services and focus settings.
