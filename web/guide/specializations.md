This page lists each Specialization, Requirement, Passive and Signature Spell/Technique


## Limits and things to know

- *A Specialization can be taken under a trial run for a day after it is chosen. Changing it is free and immediate.*
- *All Specializations require two skills to be maxed to 100 and be* [[Master Skills]] 
- *The player can only have one martial and one arcane specialization per character*

## Specializations
### Arcane
  | Specialization | Requirements | Passive | Signature Spell |
  |:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
  | Battlemage | Alteration and Destruction | **Spellsteel:** While your Shield holds, your damage spells deal 15% more damage | **Aegis Bolt:** Gives you a shield worth 2 times the damage dealt (up to 20% of your health) for 8 rounds. |
  | Bloodmage | Destruction and Restoration | **Sanguine Tide**: Your damage spells deal 1% more damage for every 2% of health you are missing, up to 25% | **Blood Boil:** If the target is below 30% health after the hit, it takes the same damage again.|
  | Elementalist | Destruction and Thaumaturgy | **Conflux:** Each of your damage spells that hits adds 3% to your spell damage for 10 seconds building up to 5 times. | **Chain Lightning:** Jumps to up to 2 other enemies within 8 meters, dealing 50% of the damage.
  | Enchanter | Illusion and Thaumaturgy | **Glamour:** +10 Personality, +15 Etiquette and +15 Streetwise | **Beguile:** If it calms the target, its magicka cost is refunded.
  | Hierophant | Restoration and Thaumaturgy | **Absolution:** When damage drops you below 30% health, you heal 25% of your health. This can happen once every 3 minutes. | **Sanctify:** Gives you 30% Spell Resistance for 6 rounds.
  | Lightbringer | Illusion and Restoration | **Dawn's Ward:** Your damage spells deal 20% more damage to undead and daedra. | **Dawnbolt:** Heals you for 20% of the damage dealt. Undead and daedra take an extra 50% of the damage.
  | Necromancer | Restoration and Mysticism | **Death's Due:** Each kill restores 5% of your health and 5% of your magicka. | **Siphon Life:** Heals you for 50% of the damage dealt.
  | Riftwalker | Alteration and Mysticism | **Between Moments:** You cast spells 20% faster. | **Rift Lance:** Other enemies within 4 meters of the target take 33% of the damage.
  | Seer | Illusion and Mysticism | **Foresight:** One enemy blow in 8 misses you. | **Psychic Lance:** For 12 seconds, the target's saving throws against your damage spells are 20 lower.
  | Shadowcaster | Destruction and Illusion | **Unseen Strike:** While you have Invisibility, Chameleon or Shadow, your damage spells deal 25% more damage. | **Shadowbolt:** On a hit, you gain Shadow for 3 rounds.
  | Soulbinder | Mysticism and Thaumaturgy | **Soul Well:** Each kill restores 10% of your magicka (20% if your Soul Trap was on the target). | **Soul Lance:** Restores magicka equal to 25% of the damage dealt.
  | Spellbreaker | Destruction and Mysticism | **Sunder:** Enemies' saving throws against your damage spells are 10% lower. | **Unravel:** On a hit, refund 33% of its magicka cost.
  | Trickster | Alteration and Illusion | **Slip Away:** When damage drops you below 33% health, you turn invisible for 5 rounds. This can happen once every 2 minutes. | **Fleeting Hold:** If it takes hold, you gain Chameleon for 6 rounds.
  | Warden | Alteration and Restoration | **Steadfast:** While your shield holds, you regain 1% of your health each round. | **Living Aegis:** For 30 seconds, enemy blows deal 20% less damage to you.
  | Wardwright | Alteration and Thaumaturgy | **Attuned Wards:** Your Alteration and Thaumaturgy spells cost 20% less magicka | **Prismatic Ward:** For 30 seconds, each Destruction spell that hits you has a 40% chance to be absorbed, if you have room for the magicka.

  | Berserker | Axe and Hand-to-Hand | **Frenzy:** Below 50% health, your blows and arrows deal 20% more damage and you swing 10% faster. | **Savage Whirl:** Hit every enemy within 3 meters. For 8 seconds, every blow or arrow you land heals you for 10% of its damage.
  | Blademaster | Long Blade and Short Blade | **Flowing Steel:** Each of your blows or arrows that land adds 3% to your damage for 4 seconds, building up to 5 times. | **Thousand Cuts:** Dash 5 meters, hitting every enemy you pass. Each enemy it hits takes an extra 50% of the damage.
  | Bloodletter | Short Blade and Axe | **Bloodlust:** Each kill restores 5% of your health. | **Gutting Lunge:** Dash 5 meters, hitting every enemy you pass. An enemy already below 25% health takes the blow twice.

  | Juggernaut | Axe and Blunt Weapon | **Stand Firm:** When damage drops you below 30% health, you heal 20% of your health. This can happen once every 3 minutes. | **Earthsplitter:** Hit every enemy within 3.5 meters. For 10 seconds, enemy blows deal 25% less damage to you.
  | Ranger | Long Blade and Archery | **Hunter's Instinct:** Your blows and arrows deal 20% more damage to beasts. | **Mark of the Hunt:** Bow: a shot that passes through up to 3 enemies.   | Strider | Archery and Hand-to-Hand | **Windborne:** +10 Speed, +15 Running and +15 Jumping. | **Windstrike:** Bow: 6 arrows rain down on a spot. Bare hands: a flying kick at an enemy up to 7 meters away, even in mid-air. Each enemy it hits restores 5% of your fatigue (up to 3 enemies).
