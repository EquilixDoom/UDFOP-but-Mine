 This page goes in to detail about Master Skills and their Limitations and Benefits.

## Limits and things to know

- *All mastered skills can be leveled past 100 to a maximum of 200*
- *Once you master a skill it cannot be changed and is **permanent** for that character.*
- *The maximum amount of Mastered skills a character can have is 6*
- *If a combination of skills are mastered it can open the possibility to use * [[Specializations]]

## Master Skills
*All skills can be leveled to 100 but only 6 can be mastered 3 Primary, 2 Major and 1 Minor.*

#### Leveling from 100 to 200 is slower
> - "**100 to 125** about 4x slower per point."
> - "**125 to 150** about 8x slower per point."
> - "**150 to 200** about 16x slower per point. This is the long term grind."
> From lattymoy, Patch 0.1.4991 

#### Mastering a skill from 100 to 200 gives dimishing returns
> "Each point above 100 gives 25% of the benefit of a point below 100. Milestones at 125, 150, 175 and 200 add an extra boost and are announced with a fanfare. A skill at 200 fights like a 140."
> 
> From lattymoy, Patch 0.1.4991

#### Magic School masteries also give benefits to their spell type alongside all of them becoming cheaper.
> "Every mastered school past 100 now strengthens its own spells, not just their price. The bonus is 1% per effective point, up to +40% at 200, the same rule as Destruction's damage."
>
> From lattymoy, Patch 0.1.8519
#### Mastery School Benefits
> - "**Restoration:** your heals heal more."
> - "**Alteration:** your Shield holds more."
> - "**Illusion:**  your invisibility, chameleon and shadow last longer."
> - "**Thaumaturgy:** your Spell Reflection and Spell Resistance hold more often."
> - "**Mysticism:** your Silence, Dispel and Soul Trap take hold more often."
> From lattymoy, Patch 0.1.8519

#### Martial Skill Benefits
> - "**Long Blade and Archery:** every blow lands up to 40% harder at 200"
> - "**Axe:** up to 80% harder against wounded foes (under half health)."
> - "**Blunt Weapon:** up to 80% harder against fresh foes (half health or more)."
>   - "Against the Oblivion Gate's court, the Elite Gates, the Remnant and the sea serpent, whose health the server keeps, the Axe and the Blunt Weapon each land up to 40% harder on every blow."
> - "**Short Blade:** swings up to 40% faster."
> - **Hand-to-Hand:** its damage already grew with the skill, and still does.
> From lattymoy, Patch 0.1.9472 also known as Martial Specialisations
