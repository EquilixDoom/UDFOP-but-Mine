DFO has the ability to load mods from a variety of sources, largely in the form of texture and audio replacements. This page will serve as both a list of officially supported mods and unofficially supported mods, as well as installation instructions according to the latest UI at the time of writing. Note that the term "unofficially supported" is chosen because the *engine* itself is capable of loading them, not because developers or the community will give you support in debugging any issues you may have with them.

Viewing your currently installed mods is easily done via the main menu's settings, or in-game by hitting escape and going to settings, then clicking the Mods & Files option at the top and scrolling down. You will find several categories including Morrowind Assets, Replacement Packs, Lighting Mod, and Texture Mods (.dfmod). These are the sections under which you can add, enable and disable mods according to your preferences.

## Officially Supported

### DREAM & DREAM 90s:

[DREAM](https://www.nexusmods.com/daggerfallunity/mods/5) and its counter-part [DREAM 90s](https://www.nexusmods.com/daggerfallunity/mods/672) are graphics mods distributed for [Daggerfall Unity](https://github.com/Interkarma/daggerfall-unity/releases) which are also compatible with DFO. These mods attempt to "enhance" the visual fidelity of Daggerfall with higher resolution, upscaled graphics that have their own artistic intent. Many users prefer to play with these mods, and support for them is available in DFO, although some issues may be present such as potential issues with Paperdolls appearances.

Additionally, with both DREAM and DREAM 90s you will also need to install the appropriate [DREAM - Mod Compatibility Patch](https://www.nexusmods.com/daggerfallunity/mods/911) or [DREAM 90s - Mod Compatibility Patch](https://www.nexusmods.com/daggerfallunity/mods/688?tab=files) for full support of 3D objects in DFO.

The following instructions walk you through installation of these textures:

**1** Open the Nexus page of either the main DREAM mod or DREAM 90s, log in and download the main files from the "Files" tab.

**2** Once downloaded, extract the dfmod files from the zip you chose. Good practice would be to then move these files to a known location, for example: ``Documents\Daggerfall Mods\Textures\`` Note that DREAM and DREAM 90s come with a number of files in their zips, all that you need for DFO are the .dfmod files contained in the "Mods" folder.

**3** Open DFO and on the main menu, click Settings and then look for the Mods & Files category. Click the category, and scroll the main page down until you see Texture Mods (.dfmod), and click "Add Texture Mod." If you have Enhanced Textures turned on, this category may already be populated and you will need to scroll further down to add more mods.

**4** Click "Choose Files" and select the dfmod for either DREAM or DREAM 90s from the directory you extracted it to in step 2. To select all of the files at once, single click the top file first and then hold shift and click the lowest mod file in the folder, then click "Upload", "Open", or "Okay" on your file picker. Once you have selected the files, they will be scanned and load the texture replacements you have chosen.

**5** Click close to return to the main Mods & Files menu, and from here you can add more files, such as the Mod Compatibility Patch for DREAM 90s, or return to the main menu or game. Note that if you added files while mid-game, you may need to reload the connection to the world for changes to take effect.

## Improved Interior Lighting

[Improved Interior Lighting](https://www.nexusmods.com/daggerfallunity/mods/144) or [Improved Interior Lighting - Fixed](https://www.nexusmods.com/daggerfallunity/mods/1305) is a lighting overhaul mod which fixes inconsistent lighting on interiors, preventing NPCs from being washed out when standing close to light sources, and generally bringing the visual fidelity closer to the exterior world of DFO with the default "Enhanced Lighting" feature enabled. This mod also contains options for whether you want objects on interiors to cast shadows or not, allowing for greater customization of interior details on a per-user basis. The differences between the original and Fixed version, as documented on Nexus, are modifications to correct for white seams caused by a spot light effect used by fireplaces, and fixing issues with missing lightsources in dungeons as well as enhancing compatibility with Darker Dungeons.

The following instructions walk you through installation of the mod:
 
 **1** Visit the Nexus mods page for either Improved Interior Lighting or its Fixed variant, log in and download the mod via the "Files" tab.
 
 **2** Extract the .dfmod file from the downloaded zip, and place it in a good known directory such as ``Documents\Daggerfall Mods\Lighting``
 
 **3** Open DFO and on the main menu, click Settings and then look for the Mods & Files category. Click the category, and scroll the main page down until you see Lighting Mod, and click "Attach Lighting Mod"
 
 **4** Click "Choose Files" and select the dfmod in the directory you extracted it to in step 2, then click "Upload", "Open", or "Okay" in your file picker. Once added, the files will be scanned.
 
 **5** Click close to return to the main Mods & Files menu. From here, you can return to the main menu and begin playing, or you can continue on to adjust the mods settings. Note that if you added files while mid-game, you may need to reload the connection to the world for changes to take effect.
 
 **6** At the top of the Mods & Files menu, there is a search bar. Click on it and type in "Improved" with no quotes. It will bring up the "Modded Lighting" option, where you can select between turning it off, turning it on, and "with shadows" which turns the mod on and enables its optional shadows. Make your selection, then return to the main menu and connect to the game to see your mods in action. Note that if you added files while mid-game, you may need to reload the connection to the world for changes to take effect.
 
### Music Packs
 
 DFO supports loading custom music packs that are distributed as loose .ogg files with the same names as the original music files. Currently, support for dynamic music packs is not included. While you can install any music pack that you wish, this guide will be recommending and guiding you through the installation of the [Daggerfall music - true-to-original high quality remake by Pieces of 8-bit](https://www.nexusmods.com/daggerfallunity/mods/314) music pack.
 
The following instructions walk you through installation of the music pack:
  
**1** Visit the Nexus mods page for your chosen music pack, login and download the main files from the "Files" tab.

**2** Once downloaded, extract the .ogg files from the zip you chose into a known directory, a good example would be ``Documents\Daggerfall Mods\Music\``

**3** Open DFO and on the main menu, click Settings and then look for the Mods & Files category. Click the category, and scroll the main page down until you see "Replacement Packs" and select "Attach Music Pack."

**4** Click "Choose Files" and select the folder that you extracted the .ogg files to in step 2, then click "Upload", "Open", or "Okay" in your file picker.

**5** Click close to return to the Mods & Files menu, you can now return to the main menu or game. Note that if you added these files while in-game, you may need reload the connection to the world for changes to take effect.

## Unofficially Supported Mods

This category is dedicated to mods that have been tested and confirmed functional by the community, though they may have issues and are not recommended by the DFO team officially. Each of the mods listed here should be tested by the author who submits the mod and confirmed to not cause game-breaking bugs.

### Paperdoll Enhanced

[Paperdoll Enhanced](https://www.nexusmods.com/daggerfallunity/mods/748) is a mod that replaces the head, body, clothing and armor textures for the paper dolls for female characters. It can give the paperdolls a slimmer and more smooth and beauty-focused appearance than vanilla.

The following instructions walk you through installation of this mod:

**1** Visit the Nexus mods page for Paperdoll Enhanced, log in and download the mod via the "Files" tab. Optionally download the "Kitty Khajiiti" and/or "Alt Genitals" files at your discretion. Note that while the mod will tell you that it depends on other mods, they are not integrated into DFO or required for this mod to function with DFO.

**2** Once downloaded, extract the .dfmod file into a good known directory, for example: ``Documents\Daggerfall Mods\Paperdoll\``

**3** Open DFO and on the main menu, click Settings and then look for the Mods & Files category. Click the category, and scroll the main page down until you see Texture Mods (.dfmod), and click "Add Texture Mod." If you have Enhanced Textures turned on, or other mods installed, you may need to scroll further to see the "Add Texture Mod" button.

**4** Select the .dfmod in the location you extracted it to in step 2, then click "Upload", "Open", or "Okay" in your file picker.

**5** Click close to return to the main Mods & Files menu, and from here you can return to the main menu to connect to the world, or back to live gameplay. Note that if you added these files while in-game, you may need reload the connection to the world for changes to take effect.
