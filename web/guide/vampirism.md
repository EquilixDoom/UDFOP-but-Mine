This page lists Vampire Bloodline, Advantage, Disadvantage and Bloodline Gift.




## Limits and things to know
- *There are a total of 9 Bloodlines each with their own Gift.*
- *Hoods when they are up allow fast travel and you take no sunlight damage.*

## Baseline
Every Vampire gains the following:
- +20 to every attribute except Intelligence
- +30 to Climbing, Critical Strike, Hand-to-Hand, Jumping, Running, and Stealth
- Levitation, Calm Humanoid, and Charm Mortal spells
- Immunity to paralysis and disease
- Spells given by Vampirism cost a quarter less to cast. 
- Take damage from the Sun and in Holy Places.
- The need to kill a living creature every 24 hours.
