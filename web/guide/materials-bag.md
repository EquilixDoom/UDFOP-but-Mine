The **Materials Bag** is an item you buy once and keep. It gives the things you gather, such as logs, ore, herbs, hides and fish, a place of their own, so they stop filling your pack. If you plan to gather or craft online, it is one of the first things worth buying.

> [!NOTE]
> This page describes the online game as of patch [[patch:0.1.8020]]. The [[topic:Materials Bag]] page in the Patch Notes lists every change to the bag itself.

## What it does

Without a bag, everything you gather goes into your pack, and a pack full of logs and ore soon gets too heavy to carry comfortably. The bag is a second list beside your pack, like a wagon's cargo, and it is kept for crafting materials only. It has its own limit of **300 kg**: plenty for a good day's gathering, but not unlimited.

## Getting one

- **Where:** every General Store online sells it, on every shelf.
- **Price:** about **500 gold**. The shop's quality, the region and your haggling move that up or down a little.
- **Stock:** the bag never sells out, and shops will not buy one back.
- **How many:** you can carry only one. Trying to buy or pick up a second says "You already have a Materials Bag."

## What goes in it

Crafting materials only: the things gathering produces, and food. A quest item, a worn item, a summoned item or an enchanted item is never a material, so it cannot go in. Food in the bag spoils just as it does in your pack.

## Where things you gather go

When you finish gathering, the goods are placed in this order:

1. **Your Materials Bag**, until it holds 300 kg.
2. **Your pack**, as far as your own carrying limit allows.
3. **Your pack past its limit.** The goods are never lost. The game tells you your pack is over its weight, so you know to lighten it.

If your bag and pack have no room for even one more unit, the game says **No room in your bag or pack** and does not start the gathering.

> [!TIP]
> With no bag, everything lands in your pack, even if that makes it too heavy. To lighten it, put materials into your Stores in any town, or buy a bag.

## Your Stores

Your **Stores** are your own storage for materials. They are kept in town: you can put materials in and take them out in any town, on its streets or inside its buildings, but not underground. Away from a town, the Stores page tells you to go to one.

Open them from the **Stores** page on the pause menu's **Holdings** tab. There you can:

- **Put in** one material, or **Put everything in** to store every storable material your bag and pack hold.
- **Take out** a material. It goes into your bag first, then your pack.
- **Empty your bag into your pack**, anywhere, any time the bag holds something.

### Materials that will not go into the Stores

Only what you gathered, or took out of the Stores earlier, can be put back. Materials you looted, bought from a shop or received in a trade stay as ordinary items in your pack. The Stores page lists them as "unstorable" and says why. You can still craft with them (see below).

## Crafting

You do not need to visit the Stores before you craft. Every crafting station spends materials from your Stores first. If they are short, the game moves the missing amount in from your bag and pack, and from your wagon, then crafts. If nothing you carry can make up the difference, it says you do not have enough in your Stores, bag and pack together. A craft that needs refining first, such as ore into ingots or logs into charcoal, plans that from your bag too.

This works at the anvil, forge, workbench, loom, mason's bench, cook fire, jeweller's bench, and the alchemy and enchanting stations, and for tempering. It covers looted, shop-bought and traded materials as well as gathered ones.

### Materials "from your pack"

When a station uses something from your pack that you did not gather yourself or take out of your Stores, the Stores page keeps it as **from your pack**. Such goods can only be used at a crafting station, or taken back out into your pack. They cannot be delivered to writs, put into the guild's Stores or sold for silver, and a piece crafted from them sells on the market for gold only. Goods you bought on the market with gold are never moved in for you.

## Rules

- You cannot sell, drop, store or give away a bag while anything is in it. The game says "Empty your Materials Bag first."
- A bag can never be traded to another player.
- The bag never holds gold.

## Finding it in your inventory

- **Enhanced inventory:** the bag opens beside your pack, in the same place as a wagon. If something else is already open there, use the **Materials Bag** button at the bottom of your pack. With no bag, the button tells you that every General Store sells one.
- **Moving things:** use **Put in bag** on a material's menu, or hold {{Shift}} and click a stack to move all of it at once, with no split box. The bag, wagon and storage panes have a search box and a category menu.
- **Classic inventory:** it has no room for a bag pane, so you reach your bag from the **Stores** page.

## Quick start

1. Buy a **Materials Bag** at any General Store.
2. Gather. Everything goes into the bag first.
3. In town, open **Stores** and choose **Put everything in**.
4. Craft. The stations pull what they need from the Stores, bag and pack for you.
